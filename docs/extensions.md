# Extensions

Android versions of four Spicetify desktop extensions: **Trash Bin**, **Play
a random song**, **Shuffle+** and **Hide podcasts**. The **Spicetify
extensions** patch adds them to the Spicetify Marketplace, on its
**Extensions** tab, each with its own switch.

Without the extensions patch installed, every switch is disabled. The
Spicetify settings page lists the extensions that are on, each with its
latest status; tap one to reopen its switch and controls.

## Trash Bin

Throws a song or artist in the trash. Spotify skips a trashed song, or a
song by a trashed artist, automatically.

Find it in the song and artist **...** menus: **Throw song to trash** and
**Throw artist to trash**, which become **Take song out of trash** and
**Take artist out of trash** once used.
Its Marketplace card has **Clear**, **Export** and **Import** for the trash
list.

## Play a random song

Plays one random song, from all of Spotify or from your library.

Find it as a **Random** pill on Home's filter row, next to **All**. Tapping
it offers a song from Spotify or from your library. Its Marketplace card
repeats both buttons.

## Shuffle+

Plays the playing playlist, album or Liked Songs in a true random order,
instead of Spotify's own shuffle.

Long-press Now Playing's shuffle button to shuffle what's playing; a normal
tap still toggles Spotify's own shuffle. A playlist's own **...** menu also
gets **Shuffle+ this playlist**, which works even when nothing is playing.
Its Marketplace card has a **Shuffle+ what's playing** button.

## Hide podcasts

Hides podcasts and episodes on Home and in Search, and the podcast chips in
Your Library. Its Marketplace card has **Also hide audiobooks**, on by
default.

Known limits: the downloaded **Podcasts** and **Books** chips under
**Downloaded** stay. Some Home shelves that the phone fills itself keep
their podcasts. A **Podcasts** filter you've already picked in Your Library
stays filtered until you clear it.
